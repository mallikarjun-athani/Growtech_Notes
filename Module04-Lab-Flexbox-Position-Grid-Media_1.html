            The one to remember
            Sticky = relative, then fixed. It behaves normally until it reaches its threshold, then it holds — and it is always bounded by its own parent, which is why a sticky section header gets pushed away by the next section.

        The comparison worth making
        Choose auto-fit, drop the item count to 2, and look. Now choose auto-fill with the same 2 items. auto-fit stretches them to fill the row; auto-fill keeps the empty columns and leaves them narrow. That is the entire difference between the two.

        What to point out
        Nothing happens between 375 and 639 — that whole range uses the base rules, written with no query at all. That is mobile first: the phone gets the simplest stylesheet, and every query adds to it.
